What We Do

Practice Areas

We offer expert legal services across the following areas.

GST Advisory, Compliance & Litigation

Our GST practice provides end-to-end support across GST advisory, compliance, audits, investigations and litigation. We help businesses manage GST implications in day-to-day operations, structure transactions efficiently, respond to departmental proceedings and resolve disputes across all stages.

  • Advise on GST implications of contracts, transactions and business models.
  • Provide guidance on classification, valuation, place of supply and input tax credit.
  • Assist businesses with GST registration, amendments, cancellation and revocation.
  • Support clients during GST audits, investigations and departmental proceedings.
  • Draft replies to show-cause notices and represent clients before GST authorities.
  • Handle appeals before the Appellate Authority and GST Appellate Tribunal.
  • Represent matters before High Courts and the Supreme Court when required.
  • Handle legacy disputes relating to Service Tax, Excise, VAT and CST.

Direct Tax Advisory & Litigation

Our income tax practice provides end-to-end support on tax advisory, compliance, assessments, investigations and litigation. We advise individuals, HNIs, NRIs, businesses and companies on complex tax matters and represent them before tax authorities and appellate forums.

  • Advise on tax implications of property sales, foreign remittances, restructuring and other major transactions.
  • Provide guidance on residential status, DTAA benefits, exemptions and anti-avoidance provisions.
  • Assist with annual tax computations, returns and supporting documentation.
  • Represent clients during scrutiny, reassessment, tax audits and departmental investigations.
  • Draft responses and representations against notices, additions and penalties.
  • Handle appeals before the Commissioner (Appeals) and Income Tax Appellate Tribunal.
  • Represent suitable matters before the High Court and Supreme Court.
  • Assist with stay of demand and representations before the CBDT on policy or procedural matters.

FEMA & Foreign Investment Regulations

Our FEMA practice provides end-to-end support for businesses and individuals involved in cross-border investments, transactions and assets. We advise on regulatory requirements, structure transactions efficiently, manage ongoing compliance and represent clients in case of FEMA violations or proceedings.

  • Advise on inbound foreign investment and outbound overseas investments.
  • Structure FDI, ODI, external commercial borrowings and cross-border transactions.
  • Assist with cross-border mergers, restructuring and liquidation of foreign-linked entities.
  • Manage reporting and compliance with authorised dealer banks and the RBI.
  • Review past transactions to identify and address delayed or missed filings.
  • Assist with FEMA compounding applications for regulatory contraventions.
  • Represent clients in FEMA adjudication and appeals before the relevant authorities.
  • Support businesses, start-ups, foreign companies, family offices and individuals with cross-border transactions.

Mergers & Acquisitions

Our M&A practice supports businesses throughout the transaction lifecycle, from deal structuring and due diligence to documentation, negotiations and post-closing compliance. We help promoters, investors, family businesses and companies manage acquisitions, investments, mergers and reorganisations while addressing legal and regulatory requirements.

  • Structure acquisitions, investments, business transfers, mergers and demergers.
  • Conduct legal due diligence, including background and public database checks.
  • Advise on exchange control, takeover regulations and sector-specific requirements.
  • Draft and negotiate term sheets, purchase agreements and transaction documents.
  • Coordinate tax, competition and regulatory considerations within the deal structure.
  • Support distressed acquisitions and corporate reorganisations.
  • Advise on post-closing integration and ongoing compliance.

Contract Drafting & Negotiation

Our contract drafting and review practice helps businesses create clear, practical and legally sound agreements aligned with their commercial objectives. We draft, review and negotiate a wide range of commercial contracts while addressing key legal, regulatory and business risks.

  • Draft and review vendor, supply, service, distribution and agency agreements.
  • Prepare licensing, manufacturing, product development and non-disclosure agreements.
  • Draft MOUs, joint venture, strategic alliance and collaboration agreements.
  • Prepare shareholders' and founders' agreements covering control, funding, exit and deadlock.
  • Include appropriate data protection and confidentiality provisions where required.
  • Review third-party contracts, identify risks and support clients during negotiations.
  • Coordinate tax, IP, competition and dispute resolution considerations where necessary.

Corporate & Commercial Law, including Insolvency (IBC)

Our corporate and insolvency practice supports businesses from incorporation and growth through governance, shareholder disputes and insolvency proceedings. We advise founders, promoters, companies, investors, creditors and insolvency professionals on corporate law and restructuring matters.

  • Assist with company and LLP incorporation, start-up recognition and equity documentation.
  • Support cap table management, board processes, shareholder matters and corporate governance.
  • Advise on shareholder and director disputes, including enforcement of contractual rights.
  • Represent clients in oppression and mismanagement proceedings before the NCLT.
  • Represent financial creditors, operational creditors, corporate debtors and insolvency professionals under the IBC.
  • File and defend matters before the NCLT and NCLAT.
  • Assist with creditor claims, resolution processes and resolution plan preparation and implementation.

Economic Offences: PMLA, Benami and Black Money Act

Our economic offences practice provides legal support to individuals and businesses facing proceedings under financial and economic offence laws. We assist clients during investigations, documentation and hearings, while protecting their legal interests before the relevant authorities and tribunals.

  • Advise on proceedings under PMLA, the Benami Property Transactions Act and Black Money Act.
  • Represent individuals, directors, promoters and businesses in investigations by the ED, CBI and SFIO.
  • Assist with statements, document production and responses to investigative notices.
  • Represent clients before the Adjudicating Authority and Appellate Tribunal.
  • Advise on provisional attachment of properties and related proceedings.
  • Assess potential exposure and provide preventive legal guidance before enforcement action.

Arbitration, Mediation & Civil Litigation

Our dispute resolution practice helps clients assess, manage and resolve disputes through negotiation, mediation, arbitration or litigation. We focus on practical strategies that address legal risks, costs and the most appropriate forum for each matter.

  • Assess disputes at the pre-litigation stage and recommend suitable resolution strategies.
  • Handle negotiation, mediation and domestic arbitration matters.
  • Represent clients in institutional and international arbitrations, including ICC and SIAC proceedings.
  • Represent clients in civil and commercial suits, appeals and tribunal proceedings.
  • Handle criminal matters arising from business and commercial transactions.
  • Represent policyholders in insurance claim disputes.
  • Assist accident victims and families in claims before the Motor Accident Claims Tribunal.

Regulatory & Compliance Advisory

Our regulatory and compliance practice helps businesses identify their legal obligations, maintain required licences and registrations, and manage ongoing compliance across applicable laws. We also provide sector-specific guidance and represent clients in regulatory proceedings.

  • Identify applicable licences, registrations, approvals and statutory filings.
  • Review compliance systems and identify potential gaps or regulatory risks.
  • Advise on sector-specific regulatory and compliance requirements.
  • Assist with customs, foreign trade, SEZ and EOU-related matters.
  • Provide intellectual property support for business operations.
  • Represent clients before regulatory authorities in notices and proceedings.
  • Support SMEs, manufacturers, importers, exporters and businesses operating across multiple states.

Digital Personal Data Protection Act Advisory, Compliance & Representation

Our data protection practice helps organisations understand and comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable rules. We advise on data processing, privacy documentation, security obligations and regulatory requirements, while also representing clients in data protection proceedings.

  • Determine whether an organisation qualifies as a data fiduciary or significant data fiduciary.
  • Review data flows, processing activities and privacy compliance frameworks.
  • Draft privacy notices, consent mechanisms and data-processing agreements.
  • Advise on lawful grounds for processing and cross-border data transfers.
  • Establish grievance-redressal and data protection processes.
  • Assist with personal-data breaches, statutory rights and regulatory inquiries.
  • Represent clients before the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Advise on related obligations under the IT Act and sector-specific regulations.
