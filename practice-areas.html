Practice Areas

GST Advisory, Compliance and Litigation

GST touches almost every business decision, from how an invoice is raised to where goods are stocked and how a contract is priced. Our GST practice supports businesses across that full cycle. On the advisory side, we review contracts and business models from a GST perspective, advise on classification, valuation, place of supply and input tax credit, structure supply chains and cross-border arrangements tax-efficiently, and assist with registration, amendment, cancellation and revocation. For businesses that want to stay ahead of the department, we also provide hand-holding during GST audits and investigations, so that officers receive accurate, well-documented responses from the first day.

When a dispute arises, we offer end-to-end representation, from the first show-cause notice right up to the final appellate forum. We draft replies and representations, appear before adjudicating authorities, file and argue statutory appeals before the Appellate Authority and the GST Appellate Tribunal, and, where the law or the process has gone wrong, take matters to the High Court by way of writ petition and onward to the Supreme Court. We also handle legacy indirect tax disputes under service tax, central excise, VAT and CST that are still working their way through the system. The service suits traders, manufacturers, exporters, service providers and e-commerce sellers facing a notice, a demand, a blocked credit or a cancelled registration.

Direct Tax Advisory and Litigation

Income tax questions rarely stay simple for long. A property sale, a foreign remittance, a change in residential status, a business restructuring or a large cash deposit can each carry consequences that are not obvious at first glance. Our direct tax practice advises individuals, high-net-worth families, non-residents, firms and companies on the tax implications of their transactions, including treaty benefits under double taxation avoidance agreements, residential status, significant economic presence, anti-avoidance rules and available exemptions. We also help with annual tax computations and documentation, so that positions taken in a return can be defended later.

When the department questions a return, we represent clients through scrutiny and reassessment proceedings, handle tax audits, and provide hands-on support during investigations. If an assessment results in additions or penalties, we draft and argue appeals before the Commissioner (Appeals) and the Income Tax Appellate Tribunal, seek stay of demand where needed, and take matters to the High Court and Supreme Court when a question of law is involved. We can also make representations to the CBDT on policy or procedural issues. This service is suited to salaried taxpayers with complex affairs, business owners, NRIs, and corporate finance teams.

FEMA & Foreign Investment Regulations

Foreign investment into India and Indian investment abroad are closely regulated under the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 and the rules, regulations and directions made under it. Our FEMA practice advises foreign companies entering India, Indian businesses setting up or acquiring overseas, start-ups raising foreign capital, and families and family offices with cross-border holdings. We structure inbound foreign direct investment and outbound overseas direct investment, advise on external commercial borrowings, cross-border mergers, restructuring and liquidation of foreign-linked entities, and help secure regulatory approvals where the automatic route does not apply.

Compliance does not end once money is received or remitted. We manage reporting to authorised dealer banks and the Reserve Bank of India, and review past transactions to identify delayed or missed filings before they become a problem. Where a contravention has occurred, we assist with compounding applications, and where the Directorate of Enforcement initiates proceedings under FEMA, we represent clients in adjudication and appeal. The service is relevant to any business or individual whose money, shares or assets cross India's borders.
